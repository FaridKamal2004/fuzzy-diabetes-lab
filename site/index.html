A STRONGER PROJECT STARTS WITH CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Explainable doesn’t mean validated.

This lab retains the coursework’s membership shapes and 40 rules. It improves engineering, error handling, reproducibility, and evaluation transparency. It does not establish medical validity or generalize to every population. Raw benchmark records are not published here.

Band labels use 0.4 and 0.6; the separate binary demonstration threshold is 0.5. Ties within 10⁻¹² of 0.5 use class 1. Missing zero-valued measurements and uncovered rule combinations produce an explicit abstention in the explorer.
